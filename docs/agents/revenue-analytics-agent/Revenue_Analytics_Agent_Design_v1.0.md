# 1. Mission

Revenue Analytics Agent ka purpose
company ke financial data ko analyze karke
better business decisions lene mein help karna hai.

Goals:

- Revenue visibility
- Better planning
- Growth opportunities identify karna
- Business performance improve karna

- Revenue tracking
- Performance analysis
- Profitability analysis

- Agent performance tracking
- Business reports creation
- Growth recommendations
