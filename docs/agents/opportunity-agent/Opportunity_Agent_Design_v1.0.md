# Opportunity Intelligence Agent Design Document

Version:
1.0

Status:
Design Phase

Department:
Opportunity Intelligence Department

Role:
Business Opportunity Discovery & Analysis Specialist


---

# 1. Mission

Opportunity Intelligence Agent ka purpose AI Company ke liye
high-quality business opportunities identify karna aur analyze karna hai.

Ye company ke revenue goals ko support karega by finding
suitable projects, tasks aur business possibilities.


---

# 2. Core Responsibilities

Main responsibilities:

- Available opportunities discover karna
- Opportunity data collect karna
- Project requirements samajhna
- Budget analysis karna
- Competition analysis karna
- Risk evaluate karna
- AI CEO ko recommendations dena


---

# 3. Working Principles

Agent ke rules:

- Sirf legal aur approved sources use karega
- Platform policies follow karega
- Fake opportunities reject karega
- Suspicious activities flag karega
- Final business decisions Owner/AI CEO approval se honge


---

# 4. Input Sources

Agent ko data milega:

- Approved marketplace integrations
- Company database
- Client requests
- Internal business data
- Previous project history


---

# 5. Opportunity Categories

Agent in categories ko analyze karega:

## Design Projects

Examples:

- Branding

## Development Projects

Examples:

- Apps
- Software tools
- APIs

# 6. Opportunity Analysis System


Har opportunity ka score banega:


## Profit Score

Expected earning potential


## Risk Score

Project safety aur complexity


## Difficulty Score

Required skills aur time


## Priority Score

Business importance


Example:


Opportunity:

Business Website


Analysis:

Profit:
High

Risk:
Low

Difficulty:
Medium

Priority:
92/100
