# 3. Proposal Structure


Every proposal contains:


## Client Overview

- Client information
- Business understanding
- Current situation


---

## Problem Analysis

Defines:

- Client challenges
- Business problems
- Improvement areas


---

## Proposed Solution

Explains:

- Recommended service
- Implementation approach
- Expected outcome


---

## Scope Of Work

Includes:

- Features
- Deliverables
- Limitations
- Requirements


---

## Timeline Planning

Creates:

- Project phases
- Milestones
- Estimated completion


---

## Pricing Section

Creates:

- Service breakdown
- Cost estimation
- Payment milestones


Rules:

Final pricing approval required.


---

## Terms Section

Includes:

- Responsibilities
- Revision policy
- Delivery conditions
